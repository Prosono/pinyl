## Design Context

### Users
Pinyl is a local home music controller for a Raspberry Pi with an NFC reader. It is mainly used by the owner in a living-room or hi-fi setup, but should be legible enough that family or guests can understand the core controls without needing the technical details.

### Brand Personality
Warm, tactile, and playful. The interface should feel like physical music hardware: hi-fi, vinyl, Spotify Connect, NFC cards, and a small hint of retro arcade energy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a dark, warm, physical UI with brushed-console surfaces, amber and green status lights, album art, record shapes, strong buttons, and compact operational panels. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, glassy neon-purple tech styling, and overly decorative marketing layouts.

### Design Principles
- Put music and device readiness ahead of raw technical data.
- Make controls feel physical, fast, and obvious.
- Use warm contrast, status lights, and readable labels for at-a-glance confidence.
- Keep admin surfaces dense but calm; this app is used repeatedly, not browsed once.
- Let retro arcade touches show up in motion, lights, and controls without turning the UI into a gimmick.
